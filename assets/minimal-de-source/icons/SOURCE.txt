Where the icons come from, and under what licence.

gentoo/  -- the gentoo file manager's icon set, Johan Hanson 1998,
   GPL-2 or later, copied byte for byte.  See gentoo/SOURCE.txt for the
   package, the paths, the checksums and what each one is used as.

That is the whole of it now.  Two sets that used to be here are gone:

   nuoveXT2/ and lxpanel/ were Debian's, vendored while this desktop was
   a copy of Debian's LXDE.  They went out with the panel: the launchers
   and the tray were the only things drawing them, and a desktop with no
   bar has no launcher bar to fill.

   drawn/ was this project's own, nine icons laid out from geometry in
   the sixteen colours.  They were flat and consistent and they looked
   like a style rather than like icons.  Drawing a thing that already
   exists, badly, is not the same as having it.
