THE MARK
========

rsd-mark.png is the RSD wordmark: the project owner's own, supplied by
him and used at his direction.

  2224 x 1008, PNG with an alpha channel.
  sha256 79973fb0f2814bfb9d148e390acc8b3027403b2296cee9af591694f28cda8788

  The letterforms are one flat colour, #7B0100 - a dark red - and that
  is the brand red everything else takes. The panel's launcher, the
  fetch's art and the website all read it from here rather than each
  holding their own idea of what red means.

The current name is RSD - Root Software Distribution - and the mark is
the wordmark above. Older unused logo files have been removed.

The ASCII the fetch prints is captured from this file rather than
traced; see assets/ascii/SOURCE.txt.
